Help & FAQ

📚 Getting Started

Click "Add Book" to start tracking a new book. Enter the title, total pages, set your reading days, and optionally add a target finish date.

📖 Logging Progress

Use "Log Progress" to record your reading sessions. The app automatically calculates pages read based on your current position.

💡 Tip: Add notes to remember what happened or your thoughts about that reading session.

📅 Reading Schedule

Select which days you plan to read when adding a book. The app will calculate how many pages you need to read per day to meet your target date.

⚡ Quick Actions

  • Log Progress: Record pages read
  • Edit: Modify book details or reading schedule
  • Export: Download your reading data as a text file
  • Delete: Remove book and all progress
  • Finish Book: Mark book as completed (100%)

📊 Progress Tracking

Your progress bar shows completion percentage. Recent reading sessions appear below each book with edit and delete options.

📱 Mobile App

Add Bukmrk to your phone's home screen for quick access. In Safari, tap the share button and select "Add to Home Screen".

💾 Data Export

Export individual book data or use the main export for all books. Files include reading sessions, notes, and statistics.

🔐 Your Data

All your reading data is stored securely and synced across your devices when you're signed in.
